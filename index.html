What this system does

Decision-first readiness review before traffic is committed.

The Certified Legendary Copy decision engine checks whether the asset is safe to test, what breaks trust first, and what must be repaired before money or attention is put at risk.

  • 1
    Launch gate logic

    Separates assets that are ready for controlled exposure from assets that still need structural fixes.

  • 2
    Claim and proof pressure

    Highlights where the copy asks for belief before it has earned it.

  • 3
    Repair sequencing

    Outputs the highest-leverage actions to address before more spend is allowed through.

Decision states
GO Structurally sound enough for controlled launch and optimization.
HOLD Potentially workable, but proof or positioning gaps still need attention.
DO NOT LAUNCH Belief, compliance, or conversion logic is too exposed for paid traffic or list attention.
Context guidance

Give the system the job, not just the copy.

Asset intent: helps separate direct response from authority or nurture roles.

Market + audience: frames sophistication, skepticism, and promise sensitivity.

Offer context: clarifies what is actually being sold so the verdict is judged against the right standard.
